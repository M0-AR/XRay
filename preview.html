Honest by design

What this kit is — and what it is not.

XRay is an interface kit. It helps you build the visible product; it does not replace your backend, your security programme or your legal review.

Interface, not a medical device

The templates display and collect data. They do not diagnose, prescribe or provide clinical decision support.

No certification claims

Nothing here is marketed as HIPAA-certified, GDPR-certified or WCAG-conformant. Compliance depends on your full system and process.

Accessibility as a starting point

Semantic structure, keyboard focus styles, labelled controls and reduced-motion support are built in. Audit your final product with real users.

Licensing

Try it freely. License it when you ship.

The repository is public so you can evaluate the work. Production use requires a commercial licence from the author. No invented prices — talk to the maintainer.
